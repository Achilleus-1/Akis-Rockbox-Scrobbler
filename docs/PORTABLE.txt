Aki's Rockbox Scrobbler — Windows portable edition

Extract the ENTIRE ZIP, then double-click Akis-Rockbox-Scrobbler.exe.
Keep _internal and the other files beside the EXE. No system Python, browser
interface, FFmpeg, or administrator account is needed.

The browser opens only for Last.fm authorization and external links.
In Settings, enter your own Last.fm API key and shared secret. Connect Last.fm,
approve access, return to the app, and click Finish connecting.

Queue, receipts, and theme settings live under %LOCALAPPDATA%\RockboxRelay.
Portable means no installation; user data stays separate from the app folder.
Replacing the app folder preserves your queue and duplicate protection.

Documentation: https://github.com/Achilleus-1/Akis-Rockbox-Scrobbler
Dependency license texts are in licenses/.
