# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss the software and ideas without harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks. Respect other people's privacy; never post someone else's private information or account credentials.

Give actionable bug reports and constructive reviews. Disagreement is welcome when it stays focused on the work. Do not spam issues, advertise unrelated services, or use this project to target another person.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive content, close inappropriate threads, and restrict participation when needed. Report conduct concerns privately through [Achilleus's contact links](https://linktr.ee/achilleus_). For software vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
